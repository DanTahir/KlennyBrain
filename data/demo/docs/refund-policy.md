# Refund Policy

This policy covers refunds for R2 Picker units purchased outright from Northwind Robotics. Support (owner: Priya Shah) is the point of contact for all refund requests.

## Refund window

- Customers may request a **full refund within 14 days of delivery** of an R2 Picker unit.
- The 14-day window starts on the delivery date recorded by ShipFast Logistics.
- Requests received after 14 days are not eligible for a refund. They may be handled as a warranty claim instead (see the Support FAQ).

## Conditions

To qualify for a full refund, the unit must:

1. Be returned with all original components: charging dock, gripper kit and safety bumper.
2. Have no physical damage beyond normal commissioning wear.
3. Not have been modified, opened or repaired by a third party.
4. Have its serial number registered to the requesting customer's account.

## Exclusions

- **Leased units** ($1,450/month lease). Lease cancellations follow the lease agreement, not this policy.
- **Atlas Fleet Manager and Cloud Console subscriptions.** SaaS fees are non-refundable once a billing period starts.
- Installation, site survey and training services already delivered.
- Units damaged in customer-arranged shipping.
- Custom gripper tooling built to a customer's specification.

## RMA process (ShipFast)

1. The customer opens a Zendesk ticket with the serial number(s), delivery date and reason for return.
2. Support confirms eligibility (within 14 days, conditions met) and creates an RMA number.
3. Support books a pickup with **ShipFast Logistics**, our robot shipping and RMA partner. ShipFast provides crating and pickup at no cost to the customer.
4. The customer packs the unit in the provided crate and attaches the RMA label.
5. On arrival at the Oakland warehouse, the unit is inspected within 3 business days.
6. If inspection passes, Finance issues the refund to the original payment method within 10 business days.

## Partial refunds

If inspection finds missing components or damage, Support may offer a partial refund minus replacement cost. The customer must approve before the refund is processed.
