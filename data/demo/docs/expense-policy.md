# Expense Policy

Owner: Elena Park, CEO. Spend company money as if it were your own, especially on behalf of customers.

## General rules

- Submit expenses **within 30 days** of purchase, with an itemized receipt.
- Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
- Expenses over **$500** need manager pre-approval in writing (Slack is fine).
- Expenses over **$5,000**, and any new software vendor contract, need exec approval.

## Travel

- Book economy airfare at least 14 days ahead when possible.
- Hotels: up to $250/night, or $325/night in high-cost cities.
- Meals while traveling: up to $75/day.
- Customer site visits (for example, robot commissioning) may use rental cars. Choose standard size.

## Customer entertainment

- Up to $100 per person. Record the customer and attendees on the expense.
- Account executives and CSMs coordinate customer spend with the account owner.

## Learning and equipment

- $1,000 annual learning budget for courses, books and conferences.
- $500 home office stipend when you join. Laptops are issued by IT, not expensed.

## Software and SaaS

New tools must pass security review (see the Security Policy) and support company SSO before anyone signs up. Do not put customer data into unapproved tools.

## Not reimbursable

Personal travel upgrades, traffic and parking fines, alcohol not tied to customer entertainment, and family travel.

## Submitting

Submit expenses in the finance portal by category. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after approval.
