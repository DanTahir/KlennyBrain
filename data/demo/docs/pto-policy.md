# PTO Policy

Owner: Tess Morgan, Head of People.

## Time off

- **Flexible PTO** with a recommended minimum of **15 days per year**. We want people to actually take it.
- **Company holidays:** 11 US holidays plus a company-wide winter break from Dec 24 through Jan 1.
- **Sick time:** take what you need. Tell your manager the same day. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, contact People.

## Requesting PTO

1. Give **2 weeks' notice** for absences of 3 or more days, and 1 day's notice for shorter ones.
2. Submit the request in the HR portal. Your manager approves it there.
3. Add the time off to your calendar and set a Slack status.
4. Arrange coverage for any customer escalations, on-call shifts or launch tasks you own.

## On-call and launch periods

- Engineers on the on-call rotation must swap shifts before taking PTO. Coordinate with Jordan Blake.
- Within 2 weeks of a major launch or release, managers may ask you to reschedule non-urgent PTO. This needs a conversation, not a blanket ban.
- Enterprise support coverage (24/7 production-down) must keep at least one support engineer available. Coordinate with Priya Shah.

## Leave

- **Parental leave:** 16 weeks fully paid for all parents.
- **Bereavement:** up to 10 days.
- **Jury duty and voting:** paid as needed.

## Disconnecting

While on PTO, you are not expected to check Slack or email. Managers should not contact you except in a genuine emergency.
