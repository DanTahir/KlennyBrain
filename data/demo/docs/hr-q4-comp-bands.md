# CONFIDENTIAL - Q4 Compensation Band Adjustment

**Owner:** Tess Morgan. **Audience:** exec team and People team only. Do not share, forward or discuss outside this group.

## Decision

- **Engineering salary bands increase by +6%, effective 2027-01-01.**
- Non-engineering bands are unchanged this cycle; revisit at the mid-2027 review.
- Employees below the new band minimum are moved to the minimum on 2027-01-01.

## Engineering bands (base salary, USD)

| Level | Title | Current band | New band (+6%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Intern / Associate | $95k - $120k | $100.7k - $127.2k |
| E2 | Software Engineer | $130k - $160k | $137.8k - $169.6k |
| E3 | Senior Engineer | $165k - $200k | $174.9k - $212.0k |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | $200k - $240k | $212.0k - $254.4k |
| M1 | Engineering Manager | $195k - $235k | $206.7k - $249.1k |

## Other bands (unchanged)

| Level | Example roles | Band |
|---|---|---|
| P2 | Product Designer, Support Engineer | $105k - $140k |
| P3 | Senior PM, Support Lead, CSM, AE (base) | $135k - $175k |

## Budget impact

- Engineering headcount in scope: 22.
- Band shift plus moving 4 people up to new minimums: **~$310k annualized** increase in base payroll.
- With benefits and payroll taxes (~22%): **~$378k** fully loaded for 2027.
- Funded from the 2027 operating plan; Elena Park approved on 2026-09-29.

## Rationale

- Offer-acceptance rate for E3/E4 dropped to 48% in Q3; three candidates cited base salary.
- Market data shows Bay Area robotics engineering bases up 5-7% year over year.

## Timeline

- 2026-10-15: managers receive individual adjustment sheets (Jordan Blake for Platform).
- 2026-12-01: individual letters prepared.
- 2027-01-01: new bands effective in payroll.
