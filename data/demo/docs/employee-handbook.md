# Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Robotics. This handbook covers how we work. Questions go to Tess Morgan, Head of People (tess@northwind.dev).

## Who we are

We build the R2 Picker warehouse robot and Atlas Fleet Manager, our SaaS fleet dashboard. HQ is in Oakland, CA. Most of the team works hybrid.

## Working hours and location

- Core collaboration hours: 10am–3pm Pacific.
- Oakland office days: Tuesday and Thursday for engineering, product and support. Other teams set their own cadence with their manager.
- Hardware lab access requires completing robot safety training.

## Communication

- **Slack** is the default. Use public channels (#eng, #support, #product, #sales, #atlas-launch) over DMs so decisions are searchable.
- Company announcements go in #general.
- Write important decisions down in the wiki (https://wiki.northwind.dev) or the relevant Linear issue.
- Customer conversations happen in Zendesk or email, never in personal accounts.

## Onboarding

1. Day 1: laptop setup, SSO enrollment and security training (see the Security Policy).
2. Week 1: meet your manager and onboarding buddy, and read your team's runbooks.
3. Week 2: robot lab safety training and an R2 Picker demo.
4. 30/60/90-day check-ins with your manager.

## Benefits overview

- Medical, dental and vision coverage starting on day 1.
- 401(k) with 4% match.
- $1,000 annual learning budget (see the Expense Policy).
- Paid time off (see the PTO Policy).
- Home office stipend of $500 when you join.

## Conduct

We expect respect, honesty and care for customers' operations. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, Tess, or anonymously through the People form on the wiki.

## Performance

Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Managers hold weekly 1:1s. Feedback should be specific and timely.
