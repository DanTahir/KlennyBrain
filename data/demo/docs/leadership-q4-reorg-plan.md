# CONFIDENTIAL - Q4 Reorg Plan (Customer Experience)

**Owner:** Elena Park. **Audience:** exec team and People team only. Not to be discussed outside #leadership until the all-hands.

## Decision

- **Support and Customer Success merge into a single "Customer Experience" organization under Ana Gomez, effective 2026-11-01.**
- **Priya Shah** (Support Lead) and **Omar Haddad** (Customer Success Manager) both report to Ana inside the new org.
- Marco Rossi continues to report to Priya.
- No role eliminations. Titles unchanged on day one.

## Why

- Customers see one journey; today escalations bounce between Support and Success (e.g. Wi-Fi drops after firmware 2.8 surfaced in tickets weeks before CS saw renewal risk).
- One owner for renewal health, ticket trends and the refund/credit policy.
- Ana already manages both leads; this formalizes a single team, budget and set of metrics.

## New structure (2026-11-01)

| Team | Lead | Reports to |
|---|---|---|
| Support | Priya Shah | Ana Gomez |
| Customer Success | Omar Haddad | Ana Gomez |

Shared metrics: net revenue retention, time to resolution, renewal health score.
