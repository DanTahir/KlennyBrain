# Support FAQ

Customer-facing answers to common questions. Send anything not covered here to support or post it in #support.

## Shipping

**How are R2 Pickers shipped?**
Every unit ships crated through our logistics partner, ShipFast Logistics. Standard lead time is 3–4 weeks from order confirmation within the continental US.

**Can I track my shipment?**
Yes. ShipFast emails a tracking link to the site contact on the order when the crate leaves Oakland.

## Leases

**What does a lease include?**
The $1,450/month lease includes the R2 Picker, the charging dock, standard warranty coverage for the lease term, and Northwind Cloud Console access.

**Can I buy out a leased unit?**
Yes. Contact your account team for a buyout quote. Lease payments already made are credited at the rate in your agreement.

## Cloud Console login

**I can't log in to the Cloud Console.**
1. Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. Reset emails expire after 1 hour.
2. Check that your account admin hasn't deactivated your user.
3. If you're locked out after 5 failed attempts, wait 15 minutes or contact support.

**Will the Cloud Console be replaced?**
Atlas Fleet Manager will replace the Cloud Console over time. Existing Console customers will get migration guidance before any change.

## Warranty

**What does the warranty cover?**
Purchased units include a 12-month limited warranty on drive motors, the gripper assembly, sensors and the main controller. It does not cover physical damage, misuse or third-party repairs.

**How do I file a warranty claim?**
Open a ticket with the serial number, a description of the fault and the robot logs (Console → Robot → Export logs). Support will troubleshoot first, then arrange an RMA through ShipFast if needed.

## Refunds

**How do I request a refund?**
See the Refund Policy (https://wiki.northwind.dev/support/refund-policy) for eligibility, conditions and the RMA process, then open a support ticket with your serial number(s) and delivery date.

## Contact

Support hours are 6am–6pm Pacific, Monday–Friday. Urgent production-down issues are handled 24/7 for Enterprise customers.
